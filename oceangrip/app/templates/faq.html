Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days. Express delivery takes 1-2 business days.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, and popular wallets like Paytm, PhonePe, and Google Pay.

Can I return a product?

Yes, please refer to our Shipping & Return Policy for details on eligibility and process.

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive tracking details via email. Order tracking is currently being added to accounts.

Do you offer guest checkout?

Yes, you can place an order without creating an account.
